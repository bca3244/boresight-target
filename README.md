# Boresight Target Generator

A web page to boresight, sight in and dial: it prints true-scale laser boresight and sight-in targets, and works out the scope clicks and a trajectory table for your firearm, sight height, load and zero range.

- Pick a rifle, pistol or shotgun, the caliber, sight height, bullet weight and shape, and the zero range.
- The page works out where the laser (which follows the bore) crosses the line of sight, and how far below the aim point the laser spot sits at a closer target range.
- Print a boresight target (Letter or A4, light measuring grid, 1 in and 5 cm bars for checking the scale), a 1-inch-grid sight-in target for the first shots, or a one-page report with a trajectory table.
- After the first shots, enter how far the group is off and get the scope clicks (MOA or mil).
- Adjust the typical velocity for your barrel length (test barrel length and change per inch are editable).
- Search the caliber list, and save named setups in your browser.
- Add your own load: a name, bullet weight, muzzle velocity and G1 B.C. Your loads are listed first under Caliber (and found by the search), and can be edited or deleted. A link to a setup carries its load, so it opens on any device; a load from a link can be saved to your loads there.
- Save to file downloads a JSON file with your saved setups, your loads, the current setup and the table, wind, shot angle and click settings; Load from file adds them back (on any device or browser). A saved setup or load with the same name is replaced.
- Point-blank range: enter the vital zone (6 in by default) to see how far you can aim dead-on, the zero that gives the longest point-blank range (with a button to use the nearest zero choice), and what your current zero gives.
- The trajectory table shows the bullet path and wind drift in inches (cm), MOA and mil. You choose how far it goes and the step (up to 40 ranges), the wind speed (10 mph by default) and the direction it comes from (o'clock), and the shot angle (up to 60° uphill or downhill, in 5° steps). With a shot angle, the ranges are line-of-sight distances (what a rangefinder reads) for a rifle zeroed on level ground; the table and range card follow it, while the boresight solution and point-blank range stay on level ground. Altitude and temperature (shared with Field) set the air the table, range card and Field holds use, for a zero made in standard air; the boresight solution and the Setup point-blank range stay in standard air. Pressure follows the standard atmosphere for the altitude, and humidity stays at the standard 78%.
- **Compare** (third tab): two loads side by side, independent of Setup. Each side has its own firearm, caliber or load (with search), bullet weight and shape, velocity and B.C. (typical, or your own), and sight height, and can be filled from the current Setup or a saved setup. Both share one zero unless B gets its own; choose how far to compare. A side-by-side table shows weight, diameter, sectional density, B.C., muzzle velocity, energy, momentum and Taylor KO, point-blank range with the zero and at its best zero, how far each stays supersonic, and how far each carries 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000 ft-lb (deer, elk, moose rules of thumb), with the higher value marked and a B − A column for the difference. A chart plots path (with the vital zone shaded), velocity (with the speed of sound) or energy (with the deer, elk and moose levels) for both, and a by-range table switches between trajectory (path, drift, time) and impact (velocity, energy, momentum, Taylor KO). Copy link shares the whole comparison (a side using one of your loads carries its numbers, so it opens anywhere), and Print comparison gives a two-page sheet: the side-by-side table and charts with a QR code, then both tables by range. Wind and the vital zone are the ones set in Setup and Field; the comparison is on level ground and never changes Setup.
- A range card below the trajectory table shows the scope clicks to dial for elevation and wind at each range, exactly as it prints (3 x 5 in, or 4 x 6 in for long tables). Its click size is shared with the click calculator, and its zoom button (like the chart's) fills the window with the card.
- **Setup and Field.** Setup holds everything above, for the bench. Field is for the range or the stand, sized for a phone and gloves: the active profile at the top (tap it to go back to Setup, or switch to a saved setup), a point-blank banner that says whether to aim dead-on at this range, a big range readout with steppers and one-tap presets, and the elevation and wind holds in scope clicks. Wind, shot angle, altitude, temperature and click size are shared with the trajectory table and range card; the point-blank banner uses the chosen air. Field never changes the profile. It keeps the screen on while it's open (where the browser supports it), warns when the bullet goes transonic or subsonic, and the page remembers which view you were on.
- Settings (the gear, top right): units, theme and paper size. Three themes: Dark, Sun (high-contrast light for bright sun) and Night (red on black, to keep your night vision).
- Works offline after the first visit, and can be installed as an app (Install app button in Chrome, Edge and Android; Share, then Add to Home Screen on iPhone and iPad).
- Every setting is kept in a short code after the `#`, so a link opens the exact same setup. Each printed sheet carries a QR code of that link. The plain address (no `#`) shows the defaults and stays clean until you change something.
- Reset all settings (in the settings menu) asks first, then puts every setting back to its default (units, paper, table, wind, shot angle, altitude, temperature, clicks, vital zone, folded cards, view, theme, the Compare loads and whether "How to use it" is open) and opens the plain address. Saved setups and your loads are kept.

Ballistics use a G1 point-mass model in the Army Standard Metro atmosphere (59 °F, 29.53 inHg, 78% humidity), or the altitude and temperature you choose for the table, range card and Field. Typical velocities and ballistic coefficients are estimates; enter your own values for best results.

## Files

- `index.html`, `css/`, `js/`: the site. No build step or server code; open `index.html` directly or host the folder as-is.
- `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`: offline copy and app install. The service worker answers from its copy and checks for a new version in the background on each visit; it switches only once every file has downloaded, then offers a Reload.
- `js/vendor/qrcode.js`: [QR Code Generator](https://github.com/kazuhikoarase/qrcode-generator) 1.4.4 by Kazuhiko Arase (MIT license).
- `tests/test.html`: engine checks. `tests/share.html`: shareable-link checks. `tests/ui.html`: caliber search, saved setups, click calculator, trajectory table, Field mode and Compare. Open any of them in a browser; the summary at the top shows the result. `node tests/run.js` runs all three in headless Chromium (Playwright), and GitHub Actions runs it on every pull request.
