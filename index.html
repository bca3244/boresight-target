Boresight Target Generator

A true-scale laser boresight target for your gun, sight and load.

How to use it
  1. Fill in the setup below, then print the target and check the ruler measures correctly.
  2. Place it at the target range shown on the sheet, with TOP facing up.
  3. Put the laser spot in the circle marked LASER.
  4. Without moving the gun, adjust the sight so it aims at the center of the crosshair.
  5. Confirm your zero with live fire; boresighting gets you on paper, not a final zero.

Works offline. After one visit the site opens without a connection. To keep it as an app, use Install app at the top (Chrome, Edge, Android), or on iPhone or iPad tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
